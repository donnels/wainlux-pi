== 9b: Height and Gap Comparison

A/B/C were one joint session on the stock base. D was a later follow-up on the same setup. Each strip was a separate job with HOME before it; the backing was not repositioned. All used power 1000 and depth 10. Software status stalled near 99% in all four runs.

[cols="1,2,1,1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Strip | Pattern | X | Height | Duration | Sean's verdict / observation
| A | 20 mm gap | 10 mm | 77.45 mm | 223 s | Deviation: bottom marker compressed.
| B | Continuous line | 20 mm | 77.45 mm | 242 s | Deviation: same bottom compression as A.
| C | 20 mm gap | 30 mm | 75 mm | 219 s | OK, approximately 75 mm.
| D | Continuous line, later follow-up | 40 mm | 75 mm | 237 s | OK; matched C.
|===

Sean's joint inspection found the gap and continuous pair matched at each height. The 77.45 mm pair deviated at the bottom; the 75 mm pair was OK. Decision: 75 mm normal Y working bound. This did not measure the mechanical hard stop or prove every multi-band placement.
